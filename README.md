# Hotel Shri Sarvatt – Hospitality Brochure Design

## Project Overview

This project showcases a multi-page promotional brochure designed for **Hotel Shri Sarvatt**, a luxury hospitality brand focused on providing comfort, elegance, and exceptional guest experiences. The brochure was created to establish a strong visual identity while effectively communicating the hotel's services, accommodations, amenities, and brand values.

The design combines premium hospitality imagery, elegant typography, and a sophisticated color palette inspired by the hotel's branding. Each page is carefully structured to guide potential guests through the hotel's offerings, creating an engaging and memorable visual experience.

---

## Design Objective

The primary objective of this brochure was to:

* Promote Hotel Shri Sarvatt's hospitality services.
* Create a visually appealing and professional marketing asset.
* Showcase luxury accommodations and amenities.
* Strengthen brand identity through consistent visual design.
* Provide guests with essential information in a clear and attractive format.

---

## Brochure Structure

### 1. Cover Page

The cover introduces the Hotel Shri Sarvatt brand using luxurious imagery, elegant typography, and the official hotel logo. The tagline **"Where Hospitality Meets Elegance"** reinforces the hotel's commitment to premium hospitality and memorable guest experiences.

### 2. About Us

The brochure includes an "About Us" section describing the hotel's mission to provide comfort, convenience, and warm hospitality. The design uses a transparent overlay and elegant background imagery to create a welcoming and sophisticated atmosphere.

### 3. Room Types

A dedicated section highlights the various accommodation options available to guests, including:

* Serenity Suite
* Tranquility Deluxe Room
* Garden Retreat Room
* The Zen Twin

Room previews allow guests to quickly explore available stay options while maintaining a visually organized layout.

### 4. Luxury Experience Showcase

This section emphasizes the premium ambiance of the hotel through high-quality interior photography and branding elements. The focus is on showcasing comfort, elegance, and attention to detail.

### 5. Hotel Amenities

The amenities page visually presents key services offered by the hotel, including:

* Coffee Lounge
* Wi-Fi Access
* Laundry Services
* Private Balcony
* Room Service
* Parking Facilities

Icon-based presentation improves readability and allows guests to quickly understand available facilities.

### 6. Contact Information

The final page provides essential contact details, including:

* Phone Number
* Address
* Email Address
* Website
* QR Code for quick access to hotel information

The QR code integration enhances accessibility and supports modern digital interaction.
